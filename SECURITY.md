# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in public issues:
- Use GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" button on the repository's Security tab, or
- Email ace.kushal.dev@gmail.com

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and the impact. This is a one-person project: expect an acknowledgement within a few days and fixes on a best-effort basis.

In scope: authentication and sessions, MFA and moderation access, hidden-data exposure, injection, account deletion.
Out of scope: findings that need a compromised device, and reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact.
